01Privacy

Who runs this site

This is the personal portfolio of Arasada Akhil, based in Hyderabad, India. For anything about your data, write to contact@arasadaakhil.website.

What is recorded when you visit

Only if you choose "Accept" in the tracking consent box, each visit records:

  • your IP address, and the approximate city, region, country and network it belongs to
  • your device type, operating system, browser, screen size and language
  • the page you opened, the site that referred you, how long you stayed and how far you scrolled
  • which sections you reached and which links or buttons you used, such as the resume download

If you choose "Decline", or do not answer, none of this is sent. You can change your answer at any time with "Tracking preferences" at the foot of the site.

Why it is recorded

To see how many people visit, where they come from and which parts of the portfolio are useful. It is not used for advertising and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies for visitors. Your browser's local storage keeps two small settings on your own device: your consent choice and whether you switched on Invert.

The contact form

When you send the form, your name, email address, reason and message are delivered to my inbox by FormSubmit, a third-party form service. I use them only to reply to you.

The MCP server

AI assistants that connect to this site's MCP address have each request logged: the time, the tool called, the name the assistant reports, its country and its IP address.

Who else handles the data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores the visit records.
  • FormSubmit relays contact form messages.
  • Google Fonts and the cdnjs network deliver fonts and scripts, and see your IP address when your browser requests them.

How long it is kept

Visit records are kept until I delete them. There is no fixed schedule. Contact messages stay in my inbox for as long as the conversation is useful.

Your choices

You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or withdraw consent, by writing to the address above. I will act on a request within a reasonable time.
